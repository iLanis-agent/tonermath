Toner Math

Boundary: arithmetic only This page counts an office print load with multiplication, division and rounding-up: the cartridges the pages take, the days a cartridge lasts, the cost a page, and the minutes the load prints. It uses no named constants. The pages, the cartridge yield, the daily pages, the cartridge price and the print speed are yours to decide and read off your own printer and invoices. It applies the counting as written, so it has not seen your printer, your cartridge or your power bill and does not know whether the cartridge will really reach its yield, the printer will jam or the price will hold, and it gives no verdict that a cartridge is worth its price or a printer is right for you. Every number here is your own entry.

The cartridges truth

Cartridges: the pages divided by the pages a cartridge, rounded up, with what the last cartridge covers.

The days truth

Days: the cartridge yield divided by the pages printed a day, with the pages left for the next day.

The cost truth

Cost: the cartridges times the cartridge price, to the cent, and spread over the pages.

The time truth

Time: the pages divided by the pages a minute, rounded up, in minutes and in hours and minutes.

Toner Math is exact arithmetic over the numbers you enter. Why it exists.
