Toner Math

The 12500 pages at 3000 pages a cartridge make 5 cartridges, the last cartridge covering 500 pages.

Boundary: arithmetic only Toner Math counts an office print load with multiplication, division and rounding-up: the cartridges the pages take, the days a cartridge lasts, the cost a page, and the minutes the load prints. It uses no named constants. It applies the counting as written, so it has not seen your printer, your cartridge or your power bill and does not know whether the cartridge will really reach its yield, the printer will jam or the price will hold, and it gives no verdict that a cartridge is worth its price or a printer is right for you. Every number here is your own entry.

Starting examples: 12500 pages, a 3000 page cartridge at $89.99, 220 pages a day and 30 pages a minute.

The days truth

A 3000 page cartridge at 220 pages a day lasts 13 full days with 140 pages left for the next day.

The cost truth

The 5 cartridges at $89.99 a cartridge come to $449.95, $0.04 a page.

The time truth

The 12500 pages at 30 pages a minute take 417 minutes, 6 hours 57 minutes.
